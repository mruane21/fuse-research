# Fuse AI Tools Scan: Sat Nov 28, 2026

No new Claude Code release since v2.1.332 (already covered Nov 25). Four picks, weighted toward the media side of Fuse for Business.

1. **[AutoClip](https://github.com/zhouxiaoka/autoclip)** (GitHub project, new: v1.6.2 shipped Nov 26, trending today). An open-source, local-first OpusClip alternative. It turns a long video (a podcast, interview, or a YouTube link) into vertical TikTok, Reels, and Shorts clips with burned-in captions, covers, and post copy, and it has an MCP server (`uvx autoclip-mcp`, [MCP guide](https://github.com/zhouxiaoka/autoclip/blob/main/docs/MCP.en.md)). For Fuse for Business, a venue could upload one long video and get a week of ready-to-post clips. You can also use it now on Fuse's own launch content. It can run on local models (Ollama or LM Studio). The Windows installer isn't code-signed, so check its SHA-256 before you run it.

2. **[Concat](https://github.com/jub0t/Concat)** (GitHub project, fairly new and moving fast: v0.2.13 shipped Nov 27). A free, open-source CapCut alternative for Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android, with local auto-captions, text-to-speech and voice cloning, background removal, and 4K export. It has no watermark and no account. It also has a built-in MCP server (`concat.exe --mcp`, [setup](https://github.com/jub0t/Concat/blob/main/docs/MCP.md)), so Claude can cut, caption, and export videos for you. AutoClip finds the clips, and Concat is the editor for brand and ad creative. It's also a reference for what a "media editor" feature in the business product could do.

3. **[Meilisearch](https://github.com/meilisearch/meilisearch) + its built-in MCP server** (established search engine; the native MCP route arrived in v1.54). Fast, typo-tolerant search with filters, facets, and geo search, either self-hosted or on Meilisearch Cloud. It could power plan-card and venue search ("tacos near Main St, open now") and campaign or media-library search in the business dashboard. The [built-in MCP](https://www.meilisearch.com/docs/getting_started/integrations/mcp) lets Claude search and inspect your indexes read-only while you tune relevance. Its MCP route is still experimental, so you have to turn it on first.

4. **[Firecrawl Local](https://docs.firecrawl.dev/quickstarts/firecrawl-local)** (new: [launched Nov 18](https://www.firecrawl.dev/changelog/firecrawl-local); this is different from the hosted Firecrawl MCP v3 covered Nov 17). One open-source Rust binary that scrapes, crawls, and maps sites into markdown on your own machine and acts as a stdio MCP server, with no credits and no Docker. It's good for pulling menus, hours, and event pages from Huntington Village venue sites to seed plan cards. It's also good for auditing a business customer's website during onboarding. On Windows it needs Rust plus the MSVC C++ build tools.

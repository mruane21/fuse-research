# Fuse: cutting Google/Firebase costs to the minimum (Nov 7, 2026)

## 0. Check your bill first (5 min)
Console → Billing → **Reports** → Group by **SKU**, filter **Project** = each of POC/Test/Prod, last 30 days. Also tick "Promotions and others" off to see the real cost.
- The top 3 SKUs are your target. Expect: **Cloud SQL (vCPU/RAM/storage)**, **Places Text/Nearby Search Pro/Enterprise**, **Place Details Photos**, **Maps JS Dynamic Maps** (web prototype), **Identity Platform SMS**.
- Maps-only view: Google Maps Platform → **Metrics** (by API + by credential) and **Quotas**.
- https://cloud.google.com/billing/docs/reports
